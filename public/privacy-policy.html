Page Doctor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Page Doctor is a web application that helps Facebook Page administrators review Page performance and generate practical content recommendations.

Information we access

When you choose to connect Facebook, Page Doctor may receive information made available through Meta's authorization flow, such as your Facebook account identifier, the Pages you administer or can access, Page name and identifier, Page tasks/permissions, and Page information and insights that Meta makes available to the app.

How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate your connection, identify the Page you selected, analyze available Page performance data, and display the resulting diagnosis and recommendations.

Data storage

The initial version of Page Doctor is designed for testing and does not provide a permanent user account system. Access information used during the authorization flow is handled by the application server for the duration needed to complete the requested analysis. We do not sell personal information.

Third-party services

Facebook/Meta services are used to authenticate users and provide authorized Page data. Meta's own terms and privacy policies also apply to information processed by Meta.

Security

We use HTTPS for the application and avoid placing access tokens in browser URLs. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable technical measures are used to protect data handled by the application.

Your choices

You can stop using Page Doctor and revoke its access through the relevant Facebook/Meta account settings. You can also request deletion of information associated with your use of Page Doctor using the instructions on our Data Deletion page.
