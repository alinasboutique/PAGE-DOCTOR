Page Doctor — Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 2 October 2026

You can request deletion of information associated with your use of Page Doctor at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email allina.muresan@gmail.com with the subject Page Doctor Data Deletion.
  2. Include the Facebook account email or other information needed to identify the connection, without sending your Facebook password or any access token.
  3. We will review the request and delete information held by Page Doctor that is associated with the request, subject to any information we are legally required to retain.

Revoking Facebook access

You may also remove Page Doctor's access from your Facebook/Meta account settings. Revoking access prevents the app from receiving further authorized data.

Confirmation

After processing a valid request, we will confirm the deletion by email where an email address is available.
